# DIRECTIONS

## Candidate A — Editorial collage

**One-line signature**: An 8rem high-contrast serif thesis spans the viewport while three torn-edge research plates overlap its baseline like an exploded scientific journal spread.

**Palette**:
- `#F3F0E8` — Archive ivory — main surface
- `#18191D` — Graphite — primary ink
- `#2148C0` — Spectral cobalt — links, active states, and primary CTA
- `#E4442E` — Proof vermilion — secondary CTA and editorial emphasis
- `#B8D8CE` — Laboratory mint — section surface and data-card fill
- `#F2C94C` — Ion saffron — highlight and diagram key
- `#8A769E` — Dusted violet — citation tabs and secondary graphics

**Typography**:
- Display: Bodoni Moda at weight 700
- Body: Archivo at weight 400
- Accent / eyebrow: IBM Plex Mono at weight 500

**Brand**: No logo or inherited color system was observed, so this direction commits to a new `SB` editorial monogram built from two cropped Bodoni letterforms plus a clean Sara Bonella wordmark. Any official EPFL or CECAM marks supplied later remain untouched and appear only as institutional endorsements; cobalt and vermilion function as the site’s new, repeatable action colors.

**Hero composition**: The opening is a pale, full-width journal spread: the proposition sits at enormous scale, with “computational physics” underlined like a marked proof.  
Three overlapping plates—a new portrait, a molecular-simulation field, and a network map—use torn paper edges, caption numbers, and clipped mono annotations rather than conventional rectangles.  
A proof-vermilion “Discuss a collaboration” tab anchors the lower-right corner while the plates drift a few pixels on scroll; reduced motion fixes the spread in its composed state.

**Decorative kit**:
- Torn research plates — hero collage, featured research, and outreach cards
- Margin coordinates — section numbers, dates, publication counts, and footnotes
- Cobalt rules with vermilion proof marks — active navigation, links, and CTA edges
- Cropped equation fragments — low-contrast fields behind research sections
- Sliding citation tabs — selected-publication filters and article metadata

**Voice angle**: Edited-journal prose with decisive thesis statements, concise explanatory decks, and mono marginalia that carries evidence without interrupting the story.

**Why this fits**: The exploded-journal structure turns a long CV into an authored scientific narrative while preserving the precision doctoral-level visitors expect. Its first screen can state Bonella’s current roles, core research territory, and collaboration action immediately, while layered evidence rewards funders, committees, and researchers who continue exploring.

## Candidate B — Y2K web revival

**Palette**:
- `#070A18` — Abyss navy — main surface
- `#F5F2FF` — Lunar chalk — primary ink
- `#5B4BFF` — Ultraviolet — primary CTA and selected state
- `#00D9FF` — Ion cyan — links, diagrams, and focus rings
- `#FF4FCB` — Positron pink — research-category accent
- `#B8FF3E` — Laser lime — statistics and status highlights
- `#281A5B` — Nebula plum — glass-panel surface
- `#6E789B` — Orbital slate — borders and secondary ink

**Typography**:
- Display: Syne at weight 700
- Body: IBM Plex Sans at weight 400
- Accent / eyebrow: Space Mono at weight 400

**Brand**: Because no existing logo was observed, this direction commits to a new kinetic `SB` orbit mark whose two letterforms share one continuous trajectory, paired with a stable text wordmark for citations and print. Official institutional marks, if later supplied, stay unchanged; ultraviolet and ion cyan become functional brand accents across CTAs, diagrams, and focus states.

**Hero composition**: A dark full-height canvas places Bonella’s name around a new interactive constellation connecting quantum dynamics, statistical physics, molecular simulation, HPC, and scientific leadership.  
Compact glass nodes reveal one-sentence research summaries on hover or focus, while a fixed lunar-chalk panel states her EPFL and CECAM roles and carries the collaboration CTA.  
A slow chromatic mesh, tiny crosshair stars, and one data ribbon supply motion without making claims of live data.  
The reduced-motion version freezes the constellation and replaces the ribbon with a fully readable static sequence.

**Decorative kit**:
- Glass orbit nodes — research areas, leadership roles, and featured projects
- Chromatic trajectory lines — connections between research, teaching, and networks
- Crosshair sparkles — focus points, link cues, and section transitions
- Capsule data ribbon — selected metrics, affiliations, and speaking availability
- Pixel-grid halos — subtle depth behind portraits and simulation imagery

**Voice angle**: Precise lab-console copy with short commands, bright human asides, and clear explanations that make advanced computation feel active rather than remote.

**Why this fits**: The networked interface makes Bonella’s computational field and institution-building role visible in seconds, giving collaborators and prospective researchers an immediate mental model instead of a chronology. Its digital energy answers the request for a cool, animated academic presence, while disciplined labels, accessible focus states, and stable content panels keep it credible for committees and funders.

## Candidate C — Italian poster × Mid-century print

**One-line signature**: A condensed vertical SARA BONELLA wordmark becomes the left-hand page spine while saturated geometric poster panels tile the rest of the viewport.

**Palette**:
- `#F4E5C2` — Semolina paper — main surface
- `#241C17` — Espresso — primary ink
- `#D33F2F` — Pomodoro — primary CTA and key headline
- `#E5AE2C` — Saffron — navigation and statistic panels
- `#809E63` — Pistachio — teaching and outreach accent
- `#2D5F7B` — Mediterranean blue — research and publication accent
- `#C45D83` — Raspberry gel — decorative highlight and event badge

**Typography**:
- Display: Barlow Condensed at weight 800
- Body: Literata at weight 400
- Accent / eyebrow: Sora at weight 700

**Brand**: With no observed logo to preserve or refresh, this direction commits to a new typographic wordmark: `SARA` stacked over `BONELLA` inside a double-rule poster frame, plus a compact `SB` stamp for the favicon. Institutional logos supplied later remain intact and visually separate; pomodoro and Mediterranean blue serve as consistent brand accents, not occasional decoration.

**Hero composition**: The first screen reads as a four-color conference poster: the vertical name spine holds navigation, a central circular aperture holds a new portrait or simulation still, and bold tiles state role, discipline, and location.  
A pomodoro rosette marked “Collaborate / Invite / Supervise” opens the contact route, while three numbered proof points surface publications, invited talks, and international programs.  
On entry, panels slide into register and the circular scientific diagram turns one quarter rotation; reduced motion presents the final locked poster immediately.

**Decorative kit**:
- Double-rule poster borders — header frame, section boundaries, and footer
- Cut-paper molecular shapes — hero aperture, project cards, and transitions
- Rosette seals — contact CTA, awards, and featured invitations
- Ticket-strip labels — dates, institutions, courses, and publication metadata
- Misregistered ink shadows — display type and selected graphic accents

**Voice angle**: Poster-sharp headlines followed by warm, literate captions—confident enough for a keynote program and lively enough for science outreach.

**Why this fits**: The modular poster tiles make a large scholarly record fast to scan, while the strong action seal keeps collaboration and invitations visible from the outset. Its European print character nods to Bonella’s Rome-to-Lausanne trajectory and her science-comics outreach without turning the site into nostalgia, giving academic authority a memorable public-facing personality.
