CECAM · since 2014

Service to a scientific community

As Deputy Director of CECAM, Sara works where research strategy meets daily scientific life: programmes, workshops, training, infrastructure, international partnerships, and the conversations that let new collaborations begin.

She describes this work as a way of giving something back to the institutions and people that help scientists grow. It also asks a different kind of research question: what structures help a distributed community do better science?

At EPFL

Teaching methods by using them

Formal results meet computation, code, and physical interpretation. The aim is not only to know a method, but to understand what it can and cannot tell us.

CH-452

Computational Methods in Molecular Quantum Mechanics

Time-dependent molecular quantum properties, density functional theory, nuclear quantum effects, and adiabatic and non-adiabatic dynamics.

PHYS-729

Non-equilibrium Statistical Mechanics Simulations

Non-equilibrium theory, computational algorithms, coding exercises, and the physics of open systems.
