02 / TWO MODES OF WORK

Researcher / institution-builder

The scientific record and the infrastructure work are not parallel stories. They reinforce one another: research sharpens the questions; networks create the conditions for more people to answer them well.

Develop methods that hold onto the physics

Bonella’s work spans condensed-phase simulation, statistical and quantum physics, molecular dynamics, supercapacitors, and magnetic-field charge transport.

Build structures that let research travel

At CECAM, EPFL, and across international networks, she connects programmes, training, infrastructure, and communities around computational science.
